# Real Craft Adventures 🧱

A kid-friendly, Minecraft-inspired game for real-world quests around Lincoln, Nebraska. Play it here:
https://karthikvepuriphoto-gif.github.io/real-craft-adventures/

- **Quest Map & Missions:** visit places, finish daily missions, earn XP and stickers
- **Workshop:** build blocky 3D creations, print them in 3D or on paper, and drive your car on the test track
- **Mansion:** explore holiday rooms and hunt for hidden gems

Progress is saved only on your own device. Have fun!
