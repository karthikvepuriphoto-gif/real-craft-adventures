🌍 How to play

🕹️ Move: drag the round stick (bottom left)
👉 Look: drag anywhere on the world
👆 Build: tap a block. 🧱 Place adds a block, ⛏️ Break removes one
🎨 Pick a block from the bar at the bottom
⬆️ Jump · 🕊️ Fly · ➕➖ Zoom
🚀 Go to any Lincoln place: Capitol, Stadium, Zoo, Airport…
🚁 Helicopter: walk up and tap Get in. Hold ⬆️ Up, stick to fly
✈️ Plane at the Airport: hold ⏩ Faster to take off, stick to steer
🗺️ Top view looks down on the city · ⭐ fly through gold rings!
🔒 Locked blocks unlock when you do real quests!
