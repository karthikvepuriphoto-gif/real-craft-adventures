🏔️ Echo Cave

An echo is sound that bounces off a hard surface — like a cave wall or a cliff — and comes back to your ears.

Sound travels through air at about 340 meters per second (about 1,100 feet per second). If a cliff is 170 meters away, your "HELLO!" takes about 1 second to get there and back!

🎶 Music Room

Sound is vibration! When you talk, sing or clap, the air wiggles back and forth, and your ears feel those wiggles.

  • Loud vs quiet: loud sounds make bigger vibrations — taller waves.
  • High vs low pitch: high sounds vibrate faster (waves squeezed together); low sounds vibrate slower.
  • People can hear from about 20 to 20,000 vibrations per second (called hertz).

Try clapping, humming low, then squeaking high, and watch the picture change!
